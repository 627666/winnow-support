Winnow — Photo Cleanup Risk Notice

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This “Photo Cleanup Risk Notice” is intended to help you fully understand the risks before deleting photos or videos. Please read it carefully before performing any deletion. By using the app’s deletion-related features, you confirm that you understand and accept the risks described here.

Article 1 — Nature of the tool

The app is an assistive tool for local photo browsing and manual organization. The app does not automatically recommend deletions or pre-select items for removal; swiping up or using a shortcut only records your manual choice. The final decision to delete is entirely yours.

Article 2 — Browsing, marking, and favorites

2.1 iCloud availability and metadata completeness may affect preview sharpness and displayed file sizes.

2.2 Swiping up only adds items to the pending deletion list and does not immediately delete them. Actual deletion requires your confirmation in the pending list and again in Apple’s system deletion dialog.

2.3 Memories and Day Memories are for rediscovery and never imply that the shown items should be deleted.

2.4 If a favorited item (♥) is added to your pending deletion list, the review screen highlights it with a heart badge and reminder, but you should still inspect it carefully yourself.

Article 3 — Irreversibility and system behavior

3.1 Deletion is performed by Apple’s system photo flow. Deleted items typically go to Apple’s “Recently Deleted” in Photos.

3.2 The recovery method, retention period (typically about 30 days, per the system), cross-device sync, and iCloud behavior are determined by the system and your Apple ID settings.

3.3 If you further empty items in “Recently Deleted,” or the retention period has passed, the content generally cannot be recovered through the app.

Article 4 — Always review before deleting

Please review each item in the pending deletion list before confirming. Be especially careful with: favorited items, portraits and family members, photos of children, travel and keepsake photos, work and study screenshots, chat records, receipts and invoices, IDs and certificates, contracts and legal documents, medical and health information, creative and design assets, QR codes containing important information, and Live Photos and videos.

Article 5 — Risks of bulk cleanup

The pending deletion list can delete multiple items at once, which increases the impact of any accidental selection. Tap to inspect uncertain items before confirming, and proceed in smaller batches when appropriate.

Article 6 — Backup recommendation

Before any large-scale cleanup, we recommend completing a trustworthy backup first — such as iCloud Photos, Time Machine, external storage, or another backup method you trust — so you can recover as much as possible in case of accidental deletion.

Article 7 — Notes on specific media

Videos and Live Photos contain motion, sound, and associated resources; an ordinary-looking cover frame does not mean the content can be deleted freely. Please play and double-check them before deleting.

Article 8 — Allocation of responsibility

The app is only an assistive organization tool and is not responsible for data loss caused by: deletions you confirm, system sync, iCloud status, device failure, mis-operation, or missing or failed third-party backups. The allocation of responsibility is further set out in the disclaimer and limitation-of-liability clauses of the Terms of Service.

Article 9 — Your acknowledgment

By continuing to use deletion-related features, you confirm that you understand deletion may be irreversible and that you will review your pending list and take sole responsibility for the deletion results.

影萃 照片清理风险提示

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

本《照片清理风险提示》旨在帮助你在删除照片或视频前充分理解相关风险。请在执行任何删除前认真阅读。使用本应用的删除相关功能，即表示你已理解并接受本提示所述风险。

第 1 条 工具性质

本应用是本地照片浏览与手动整理辅助工具。本应用不会自动给出删除推荐，也不会自动挑选待删除项目；上滑或按快捷键仅记录你的手动选择，最终是否删除完全由你自行决定。

第 2 条 浏览、标记与收藏

2.1 iCloud 下载状态与元数据完整性可能影响预览清晰度及文件体积显示。

2.2 上滑仅将项目加入待删除清单，不会立即删除；真正删除须由你在待删除清单中确认，并再次通过 Apple 系统照片删除确认框。

2.3 拾光回忆与当日回忆均为随机回顾，不代表所展示的内容应当删除。

2.4 若你误将已收藏（♥）的照片加入了待删除清单，复核页会以爱心标识与提醒提示你确认，但仍请你亲自核对。

第 3 条 删除的不可逆性与系统行为

3.1 删除由 Apple 系统照片流程执行。删除后的项目通常进入 Apple「照片」的「最近删除」。

3.2 恢复方式、保留期限（通常为约 30 天，以系统为准）、跨设备同步与 iCloud 行为，均由系统与你的 Apple ID 设置决定。

3.3 若你在「最近删除」中继续清空，或保留期已过，相关内容通常将无法通过本应用恢复。

第 4 条 删除前务必复核

请在确认删除前逐张复核待删除清单，对以下重要类型尤其谨慎：已收藏的照片、人像与家人、孩子照片、旅行与纪念照片、工作与学习截图、聊天记录、票据与发票、证件与证明、合同与法律文件、医疗与健康信息、创作与设计素材、含重要信息的二维码，以及实况照片与视频。

第 5 条 批量清理风险

待删除清单支持一次性删除多项内容，单次提交数量越多，误删影响越大。建议你在确认删除前放大核对不确定的项目，必要时分批、小步整理。

第 6 条 备份建议

在进行较大规模清理前，建议你先完成可信备份，例如 iCloud 照片、Time Machine、外部存储或其它你信任的备份方案，以便在误删时尽可能恢复。

第 7 条 媒体特别提示

视频与实况照片（Live Photo）包含动态画面、声音与关联资源；静态封面帧看似普通不代表其内容可随意删除。删除此类内容前请额外播放确认。

第 8 条 责任界定

本应用仅作为整理辅助工具，不对因下述情形造成的数据丢失承担责任：你确认的删除、系统同步、iCloud 状态、设备故障、误操作，或第三方备份缺失或失效。相关责任界定另见《服务条款》中的免责声明与责任限制条款。

第 9 条 你的确认

继续使用删除相关功能，即表示你确认：你理解删除可能不可逆，并将亲自复核待删除清单、对最终删除结果自行承担责任。
