Winnow — Subscription Details

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This “Subscription Details” explains the benefits, free deletion quota rules, billing, and management of the free version and Pro in Winnow (also “the app”). It is part of the Terms of Service. All prices, periods, and offers are ultimately as shown in real time on the App Store purchase page for your region.

Article 1 — Plan overview

1.1 The app uses a “Free + Pro” model. Pro can be obtained through an auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

1.2 The photo wall, All Photos, Memories, Day Memories, and Favorites sync — all browsing and appreciation features — are fully open to everyone and are not limited by Pro status.

1.3 The core difference between Free and Pro is the number of deletions allowed.

Article 2 — Free version quota rules

2.1 The free version has full access to all browsing and favorite features.

2.2 New users receive a one-time welcome allowance of 100 free deletions (the “welcome quota”), which does not reset monthly and remains available until used up.

2.3 In addition to the welcome quota, the free version includes up to 15 free deletions per calendar month (the “monthly quota”), which resets automatically at midnight on the 1st of each month (in your device’s local time zone) and does not roll over.

2.4 When you delete on the free plan, the monthly quota is consumed first, and any remainder is deducted from your welcome quota so your non-expiring welcome allowance lasts as long as possible.

2.5 Quotas are deducted only after a deletion succeeds; if you cancel in the system deletion dialog, no quota is consumed.

2.6 Quota tracking stores only the calendar month, monthly count used, and remaining welcome count locally on your device — no photo information is stored or uploaded.

2.7 When the number you choose to delete exceeds your total remaining free quota, the app blocks the entire batch (deleting none) and prompts you to keep some items or upgrade to Pro.

Article 3 — Pro benefits

3.1 Pro removes the deletion limit, allowing unlimited deletions across iPhone and Mac under the same Apple ID.

3.2 Pro does not change the system behavior of deletion: deletion is still performed by Apple’s system photo flow and requires your confirmation.

3.3 Pro obtained via an auto-renewing subscription provides these benefits while active; Pro obtained via lifetime purchase is long-term and requires no renewal.

Article 4 — Products and pricing

4.1 The app offers three purchase options: a monthly auto-renewing subscription, a yearly auto-renewing subscription, and a one-time “Lifetime Unlock.”

4.2 “Lifetime Unlock” is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase; it does not auto-renew or bill periodically. Its entitlement is tied to the Apple ID used to purchase and can be synced to your other devices via “Restore Purchases.”

4.3 The name, price, billing period, and applicable taxes of each product are as shown in real time on the App Store purchase page for your region.

4.4 Prices may vary by region, exchange rate, or Apple policy.

Article 5 — Billing and auto-renewal

5.1 Once a purchase is confirmed, payment is charged to your Apple ID.

5.2 Monthly and yearly plans are auto-renewing subscriptions. Unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period, a subscription renews automatically at the selected period and price.

5.3 Renewal charges are billed via your Apple ID within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

5.4 The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and does not auto-renew.

Article 6 — Cancellation and management

6.1 You can view, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel a subscription at any time via “Winnow Pro → Manage Subscriptions” in the app, or in System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

6.2 Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period; until then you retain Pro access.

6.3 Canceling does not automatically refund; a billing period already started is generally not prorated unless required by applicable law.

Article 7 — Refunds

Purchases and refunds for the app are handled by Apple under its policies. The developer cannot process refunds directly. To request a refund, use Apple’s “Report a Problem” (reportaproblem.apple.com) or App Store channels.

Article 8 — Restore purchases

If you switch devices, reinstall the app, or restore your system, use “Restore Purchases” in Settings or on the Winnow Pro screen to re-sync your Pro entitlement via your Apple ID. If Apple’s transaction status is delayed, the entitlement takes effect once the App Store completes verification.

Article 9 — Offer codes and promotions

We may occasionally distribute official App Store Offer Codes or limited-time promotions. You can redeem valid codes via “Winnow Pro → Redeem Offer Code” inside the app or in the App Store. Eligibility, discounted pricing, promotional duration, and post-offer renewal terms are always as shown on the App Store redemption sheet.

Article 10 — Family Sharing

The app’s subscriptions and lifetime unlock are not currently enabled for Family Sharing. Entitlements are tied to the Apple ID used to purchase.

Article 11 — Price and benefit changes

11.1 We may adjust prices or plan composition. For existing subscribers, price changes are handled per Apple’s rules — typically with advance notice and, where required, your confirmation before renewal continues.

11.2 We may adjust the welcome quota, monthly deletion quota, or benefit boundaries; changes take effect after being updated in the app and in these details.

Article 12 — Deletion responsibility

Whether on Free or Pro, deletion cannot be undone by the app, and the responsibility to review before deletion is always yours. See the “Photo Cleanup Risk Notice.”

Version & updates: This document is dated November 23, 2026. We may update it from time to time; the latest version is shown in the app, and material changes are announced in-app. Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated version.

影萃 订阅说明

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日

本《订阅说明》解释影萃（以下简称「本应用」）的免费版与 Pro 的权益、免费删除额度规则、计费与管理方式。本说明是《服务条款》的组成部分。所有价格、周期与优惠最终以你所在地区 App Store 购买页实时展示为准。

第 1 条 方案概述

1.1 本应用采用「免费版 + Pro」模式。Pro 权益可通过自动续期订阅获得，也可通过一次性永久买断获得。

1.2 照片墙、全部照片、拾光回忆、当日回忆与收藏同步等全部浏览与欣赏功能，对所有用户完全开放，不因是否为 Pro 而受限。

1.3 免费版与 Pro 的核心区别在于可执行的删除张数上限。

第 2 条 免费版额度规则

2.1 免费版可完整使用全部浏览与收藏功能。

2.2 新用户首次体验赠送 100 张一次性免费删除额度（「新手体验额度」），该额度不按月清零，直到消耗完毕为止。

2.3 除新手体验额度外，免费版每个自然月另享有 15 张免费删除额度（「每月删除额度」），每月 1 日 0 点（以设备本地时区为准）自动重置，不累计结转至下月。

2.4 执行删除时，系统优先扣减当月剩余的「每月删除额度」，超出部分再扣减「新手体验额度」，最大限度保留你的永久体验额度。

2.5 删除计数仅在系统确认并删除成功后扣减；若你在系统删除确认框中点击取消，不扣减任何额度。

2.6 额度记录仅在设备本地保存「自然月、当月已用月度额度与新手剩余额度」，不含任何照片信息，也不会上传。

2.7 当本次选择删除的数量超过当前可用总免费额度时，本应用会整批拦截、不删除任何一张，并提示你保留部分项目或升级 Pro。

第 3 条 Pro 权益

3.1 Pro 解除删除上限，可不限量删除，并支持同一 Apple ID 在 iPhone 与 Mac 上通用。

3.2 Pro 不改变删除的系统行为：删除仍由 Apple 系统照片流程执行并需你确认。

3.3 通过自动续期订阅获得的 Pro，在订阅有效期内享有上述权益；通过永久买断获得的 Pro 为长期有效，无需续费。

第 4 条 商品与价格

4.1 本应用提供三种购买方式：月度自动续期订阅、年度自动续期订阅，以及一次性「永久解锁」买断。

4.2 「永久解锁」为一次性的非消耗型内购（non-consumable），购买后不自动续费、不按周期扣款；其权益与购买所用的 Apple ID 绑定，可通过「恢复购买」在你的其它设备上同步。

4.3 各商品的名称、价格、计费周期与适用税费，以你所在地区 App Store 购买页实时展示为准（例如中国大陆地区当前参考价为月度 ¥18、年度 ¥28、永久买断 ¥68；其它国家或地区按当地 App Store 币种与定价层级展示）。

4.4 价格可能因地区、汇率或 Apple 政策不同而异。

第 5 条 计费与自动续期

5.1 购买确认后，费用将记入你的 Apple ID。

5.2 月度与年度订阅为自动续期订阅。除非在当前计费周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，订阅将按所选周期与价格自动续费。

5.3 续费费用将在当前周期结束前 24 小时内通过你的 Apple ID 扣取。

5.4 永久买断为一次性购买，不会自动续期。

第 6 条 取消与管理

6.1 你可随时在应用内「影萃 Pro → 管理订阅」或系统「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中查看、升级、降级或取消订阅。

6.2 取消将在当前已付费周期结束后生效；在此之前你仍可继续使用 Pro 权益。

6.3 取消订阅不会自动退款，已开始的当前周期通常不予按比例退款，除非适用法律另有规定。

第 7 条 退款

本应用的购买与退款由 Apple 依其政策处理。开发者无法直接为你办理退款。如需申请退款，请通过 Apple 的「报告问题」（reportaproblem.apple.com）或 App Store 渠道提出。

第 8 条 恢复购买

若你更换设备、重装应用或恢复系统，可在应用内「设置 → 恢复购买」或「影萃 Pro」页面中使用「恢复购买」，通过你的 Apple ID 重新同步 Pro 权益。若 Apple 交易状态存在延迟，权益可能需要等待 App Store 完成验证后生效。

第 9 条 优惠码与促销

我们可能不定期通过 App Store 发放官方优惠码（Offer Codes）或限时促销优惠。你可在应用内「影萃 Pro → 兑换优惠码」或 App Store 中输入有效优惠码进行兑换。优惠码的适用条件、折后价格、优惠期及到期后的续订规则均以 App Store 兑换页面实时展示为准。

第 10 条 家庭共享

本应用当前订阅与永久解锁未开启家庭共享（Family Sharing）。权益与购买所用的 Apple ID 绑定。

第 11 条 价格与权益变更

11.1 我们可能调整价格或方案构成。对于已订阅用户，价格变更将依据 Apple 的规则处理，通常会在生效前通知，必要时需要你确认后方可继续续订。

11.2 我们可能调整免费版新手体验额度、每月删除额度的具体数值或权益边界；调整将在应用内与本说明中更新后生效。

第 12 条 删除责任提示

无论免费版或 Pro，删除均不可由本应用撤销，删除前的复核责任始终在你。详见《照片清理风险提示》。
