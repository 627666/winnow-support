FAQ

Is my data private? / 我的数据安全吗?

Yes. All recognition and scoring run on your device using Apple's on-device intelligence. Your photos and the analysis results never leave your device and are never uploaded.

是的。所有识别与评分都在本机用 Apple 端侧智能完成,照片与分析结果绝不离开设备、绝不上传。

How many photos can I delete? / 能删多少张?

The free version lets you delete up to 10 photos per day. Pro removes the daily limit — available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock.

免费版每天最多删除 10 张;Pro 不限量,提供按月/按年订阅或一次性永久解锁。

How do I restore a purchase? / 如何恢复购买?

Open Settings inside the app and tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

在应用内「设置」中点击「恢复购买」,并确认登录的是当初购买所用的 Apple 账户。

Does it work with iCloud Photos? / 兼容 iCloud 吗?

Yes. With Optimize Storage enabled, Winnow analyzes the local version and will not download your originals just to scan.

兼容。开启「优化储存」时,影萃只分析本机版本,不会为扫描而下载原片。
