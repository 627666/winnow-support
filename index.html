FAQ

Is my data private? / 我的数据安全吗？

Yes. Everything runs locally on your device using Apple’s PhotoKit and on-device system frameworks. Winnow has no developer servers, no accounts, no ads, and no tracking SDKs — your photos never leave your device.

是的。所有功能都在你的设备本地通过 Apple PhotoKit 等系统框架运行。影萃没有开发者服务器、无账号、无广告、无第三方追踪 SDK，照片绝不离开设备。

How many photos can I delete on the free plan? / 免费版能删多少张？

All browsing, memories, photo wall, and favorites features are completely free. New users receive a one-time welcome allowance of 100 free deletions (which never expires monthly), plus 15 free deletions every calendar month (resetting on the 1st of each month; monthly quota is used first). Winnow Pro removes all deletion limits — available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock across iPhone and Mac.

照片墙、全部照片、拾光回忆、当日回忆与收藏同步均永久免费开放。新用户首次赠送 100 张永久体验删除额度，另享每月 15 张免费删除额度（次月 1 日自动重置，优先消耗当月额度）。影萃 Pro 解除删除上限、不限量清理，支持按月/按年订阅或一次性永久买断，iPhone 与 Mac 通用。

Do favorites sync with Apple Photos? / 收藏会同步到苹果「照片」吗？

Yes. Favoriting a photo in Winnow updates Apple Photos directly via PhotoKit (and syncs via iCloud Photos if enabled on your device). Changes made in Apple Photos also sync back immediately.

会的。在影萃中点击收藏（或按 F 键）会直接通过 PhotoKit 同步到系统「照片」App（开启 iCloud 照片时同步至其他设备），在系统「照片」中修改收藏也会实时回流同步。

How do I restore a purchase or redeem an offer code? / 如何恢复购买或兑换优惠码？

Open Settings inside the app and tap “Restore Purchases” (or open the Winnow Pro sheet to tap “Restore Purchases” / “Redeem Offer Code”). Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

在应用内「设置」或「影萃 Pro」页面中点击「恢复购买」或「兑换优惠码」，并确认当前登录的是购买或兑换所用的 Apple 账户。

Does it work with iCloud Photos? / 兼容 iCloud 照片吗？

Yes. With Optimize Storage enabled, Winnow displays local previews and lets Apple’s Photos system fetch full-resolution originals on demand only when you view them.

兼容。开启「优化储存」时，影萃优先使用本地预览，仅在你真正浏览原片时由 Apple 照片系统按需读取，不会为了展示照片墙而提前下载全部原片。
